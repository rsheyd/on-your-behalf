# On Your Behalf Extended Profile Field Guide

This guide is intentionally comprehensive, but your profile does not need to be. Think of the sections below as a menu of ideas—not a required form. Use any structure that makes sense to you, keep only facts you expect forms to ask for, and leave out anything you do not want to share.

You can rename, reorder, combine, or delete sections. You can also ignore the outline entirely and write naturally. On Your Behalf (OYB) works with ordinary text; it does not require these headings or this exact format.

## Quick start

1. Copy any useful sections into your profile document.
2. Delete sections and blank items you do not need.
3. Add only information you are comfortable sending to your selected AI provider.
4. **Before importing, delete this entire guide—including everything above the “My OYB Profile” heading.** Otherwise, the guide will become part of your OYB profile too.
5. In Google Docs, choose **File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx)**.
6. Open OYB and select **Settings**.
7. Under **Your profile**, choose the downloaded `.docx` file.
8. Review the imported text, make any needed edits, and select **Save settings**.

OYB stores the imported profile in Chrome extension-local storage. When you ask OYB to fill a form, profile text may be sent directly to your selected AI provider.

## Bring information from other software

If you already use a browser autofill tool, password manager, form filler, contacts app, resume builder, or similar software, it may offer a way to view, copy, or export your saved personal information. You can paste relevant, non-secret facts into the **Imported information** section below and organize them later—or leave them there as ordinary text.

Export formats and available fields vary by product. Prefer copying only the specific profile information you want OYB to use. Do not import a complete password-manager vault or raw export: it may contain passwords, payment details, recovery codes, private notes, and other secrets. If an export contains mixed data, open it locally and copy only the safe, relevant fields into this document.

## Privacy and safety

Personal and demographic details can be sensitive. Include them only if you expect to use OYB for forms that require them.

Do not include passwords, authentication or recovery codes, payment-card or bank details, Social Security or tax identification numbers, passport or driver's-license numbers, or other secrets. Include another person's contact information only with their permission.
