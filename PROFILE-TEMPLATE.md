# On Your Behalf Starter Profile

This is a short starting point. Use any structure you like, skip anything that is not useful, and include only facts you are comfortable sharing with your selected AI provider. OYB and the selected AI provider will use this information for intelligently filling in online forms.

Before importing this document into On Your Behalf (OYB), delete this introduction and any blank prompts. Do not include passwords, authentication or recovery codes, financial information, government identification numbers, or other secrets.

To import from Google Docs:

1. Choose **File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx)**.
2. Open OYB and select **Settings**.
3. Under **Your profile**, choose the downloaded `.docx` file.
4. Review the imported text, make any needed edits, and select **Save settings**.

Looking for more ideas? See the [extended profile field guide](https://github.com/rsheyd/on-your-behalf/blob/main/PROFILE-FIELD-GUIDE.md).
